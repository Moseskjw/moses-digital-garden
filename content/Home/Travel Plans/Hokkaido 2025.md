The party for the trip consisted of 2 Elderly (60+ ); 2 Adults (30+) and 1 Child (3)

## Overview

Ten-day self-drive trip starting and ending at New Chitose Airport. The car is collected on arrival. The route goes south-west to Niseko, down to Hakodate, back up via Lake Toya, and finishes with five nights based in Sapporo.

**Route:** Chitose → Niseko → Hakodate → Lake Toya → Sapporo → Chitose → Changi
